Task 3(d) Comparisons and Explanations:
1. Baseline vs Tuned Mean CV RMSE:
   - The linear baseline yielded a Mean CV RMSE of approximately 10.20 MPa.
   - The tuned polynomial Ridge model achieved a much lower Mean CV RMSE of approximately 6.09 MPa because polynomial features capture non-linear relationships.

2. Example of a Degree-2 Interaction Term:
   - An example of an interaction term in a degree-2 model with features like Cement (x1) and Water (x2) is (x1 * x2), which models how ingredients work together.

3. Why Scaling Belongs Inside the Pipeline:
   - StandardScaler scales features to mean 0 and variance 1. Putting scaling inside the Pipeline ensures that scaling is dynamically fitted only on training folds during cross-validation, completely preventing data leakage from validation or test sets.
